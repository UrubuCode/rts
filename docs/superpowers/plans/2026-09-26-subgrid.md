# Subgrid (CSS Grid Level 2 §9) — the tracks come from the parent

**Written 2026-09-26** from the tree at `3c3bf082f`, from the css-grid triage
(cause 3) and a survey of `layout/grid/` (1 255 lines) and `style/`. Rules:
`crates/rts-dom/PLAN.md` §1–§2, `docs/ui/html-engine/box-tree.md` §7.

## What is true today, measured

- `grid-template-columns: subgrid` does not parse: `GridTrack::parse_one`
  (`style/values/grelha.rs:~103`) has no arm for the keyword, `parse_list`
  answers `None` for the whole property, and `grid.rs:44` falls back to one
  `1fr` column — the keyword is ignored and the box is an ordinary grid.
  That is why 33 of the 98 `subgrid/` reftests pass: coincidence, plus four
  parsing-only tests that pass for the invalid cases by the same accident.
- `<line-names>` (`[a b]`) in a track list do not parse either
  (`split_top_level` makes `[a]` one token that no arm accepts and the list
  drops); only the implicit `X-start`/`X-end` names from
  `grid-template-areas` exist (`style/grid_areas.rs`). GRID-3 found this.
- A nested grid lays out its own tracks from scratch: `block/block.rs:662`
  dispatches to `layout_children_grid` from `effective_display()` alone,
  with `content_w` and `container_content_h` as its only inputs — nothing
  carries an ancestor's resolved lines, names or gap. The intrinsic width
  of a grid (`measure/tree.rs:142`, the "wrap" branch) is the sum of its
  children's, never its template.
- The 64 failing files by need: baseline across the subgrid 18, line names
  through the subgrid 16, gap inheritance 16, one-axis subgrid 13,
  auto-fill against inherited tracks 9, orthogonal writing modes 6 (the
  writing-mode plan), MBP of the subgrid in the outer sizing 4, two-axis
  ≥ 6, nested 2, abspos 2, parsing-only 4, singles 9.

## The FORM, fixed first

**F1. The template value is a type, and `subgrid` is one of its variants.**
`style/values/grelha.rs` gains

```rust
pub enum GridTemplate { None, Tracks(TrackList), Subgrid(Vec<LineNames>) }
pub struct TrackList { pub entries: Vec<(LineNames, GridTrack)>, pub trailing: LineNames }
```

so `<line-names>` finally exist in a track list (before each track and after
the last), `repeat()` carries them too, and `getComputedStyle` round-trips
the three forms. Every reader of `grid_template_columns/rows` (`grid.rs`,
`tracks.rs`, `collapse.rs`, `aspect.rs`, the serialiser) is repointed in the
same lot; the placement's `named_line` (GRID-3) resolves explicit names
before the implicit area names, per §7.2.

**F2. A subgridded axis borrows the parent's lines through ONE seam:
`Subgridded { positions: Vec<f32>, names: Vec<LineNames>, gap: f32 }`,
handed to `layout_children_grid` by the parent's placement of that item.**
The parent knows the item's area (`axis_placement`) and its resolved track
positions; it passes the slice spanning the area, translated to the item's
border box, with the parent's gutter. In that axis the subgrid does not
resolve tracks: its lines ARE the given positions (minus its own margin,
border and padding at the two edges, §9.3 "the subgrid's edges are inset by
its MBP"), its gap is the parent's, its line names are the parent's for
that span plus its own `subgrid [names]` list, and `repeat(auto-fill)` in
its own template counts against the borrowed track count. The other axis is
an ordinary grid. Two-axis subgrid is the same seam twice.

**F3. Sizing flows up through the same seam.** §9.3: a subgrid's items
contribute to the PARENT's track sizing as if they were the parent's items
spanning the mapped tracks, with the subgrid's MBP added to the items at
its edges. `tracks::resolve_tracks` receives those contributions from a
collector that walks into subgridded children (`grid/subgrid.rs`,
new) before sizing — one recursion for nested subgrids, which by
construction resolve after their ancestor.

**F4. What does not change:** `layout_children_grid`'s dispatch from
`block.rs`, the placement algorithm, `aspect.rs`. A grid item that is a
normal grid inside a subgrid keeps laying out alone
(`independent-tracks-from-parent-grid`).
