# BT-2: layout returns fragments — implementation plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** Layout stops writing into a shared, position-indexed list and returns fragments keyed by box, so that the paint order is a traversal of a tree rather than arithmetic on vector indices, and the fragments of a split inline exist as fragments instead of being collapsed to their union the moment they are computed.

**Spec:** `docs/ui/html-engine/box-tree.md` §2 (the decision: two trees), §7 (invariants I1, I4, I5, I7, I9) and §8 phase 2. `crates/rts-dom/PLAN.md` §9 BT-2 is the lot; §0 is the state.

**Written 2026-09-25**, from the tree at `36c1545d2` (main), after reading every site named below. What was measured before writing is in "The state this plan starts from".

## The FORM, fixed first

The lesson of BT-3 (issue #2731, comment of 2026-09-16): when a lot splits, the form that unites the parts is fixed before anyone writes. This is the form. **A sub-lot that needs a different one stops and says so.**

1. **A box is mandatory everywhere layout runs.** `layout_block` and everything under it take a `BoxId`, never an `Option<BoxId>`. There is no "list without a tree" — `DisplayList::default()` has no non-test caller — and a DOM child that generates no box (a comment) is handled by the flow SEQUENCE before it reaches a layout function, never by a `None` threaded through fourteen signatures.

2. **The output of a container is a SEQUENCE of pieces, not two vectors joined by an index.** `DisplayList { items: Vec<DisplayItem>, children: Vec<ChildRef> }` where `ChildRef::at` says "before item N" is replaced by one ordered `Vec<Piece>` with `Piece::Item(DisplayItem) | Piece::Child(ChildRef)`. Paint order is the order of the vector; nesting is the `ChildRef`. `at`, `hit_at`, `filhos_antes`, `filhos_dentro`, `insert_item` and the index-fixing loops of `empilhamento.rs` and `relativo.rs` are deleted, not ported. `BeginClip`/`EndClip` stop counting children: what is between them in the sequence is inside the clip.

3. **The hit-test order is derived, never stored beside the paint order.** `hit_order: Vec<BoxId>` is a second sequence that grows for different reasons than `items` and has already cost one z-order defect (`fragmento_tipos.rs`, `hit_at`). It becomes a traversal of the piece sequence at `geometry()` time: a `Piece::Rect { box }` marker where a box records its geometry is what the traversal reads. One sequence, two readers.

4. **A fragment is the unit a container hands back, and it stays keyed by `BoxId`.** Nothing changes in `Fragment`'s identity, cache key (`BoxCacheTarget`), remap on the way in, or the stitch — those are BT-1's and are done. What changes is that `items`/`children`/`hit_order` become `pieces`, and `emit_at` appends ONE `Piece::Child`.

5. **The fragments of a split inline exist as such.** `union_box_rect` (one rect per box, grown line by line) becomes one rect per FRAGMENT of the box: `Fragment.rects: Vec<(BoxId, Rect)>` may hold the same `BoxId` several times. `DisplayList::rect_of` and `rect_of_box` union at the boundary the bridge promises (four numbers per `NodeId`); `Geometry::rects` keeps the hit-test rect per box as it is today. `getClientRects` becomes possible and is NOT added here.

6. **Every sub-lot is measured on its own, and the first two are zero-gain.** The rulers are in "Rulers". A gain in BT-2a or BT-2b is the sign that something changed that was not meant to — stop and find out what.

What this plan does NOT decide: stacking contexts painted recursively (SC, depends on this lot and is its own), `getClientRects`, anonymous-box geometry reachable from the DOM (they have no `NodeIdx`; that is the bridge's contract, unchanged).

### BT-2b — the paint order is a sequence (I5 dies)

Zero lost, **zero gained**, and the paint dump byte-identical.

**Files:** `display.rs` (the type), `fragmento_tipos.rs`, `fragmento.rs` (emit/stitch), `itens.rs` (walk), `empilhamento.rs`, `relativo.rs`, `inline_fragmentos.rs`, `fundo_imagem.rs`, `linha.rs`, `bloco.rs`, `table/mod.rs`. New: `layout/pecas.rs` (the `Piece` type and the two traversals: paint, hit order).

- [ ] **Task 1: `Piece` and one sequence.** `DisplayList.items` + `.children` → `pieces: Vec<Piece>`; `Fragment.items` + `.children` + `.hit_order` → `pieces: Rc<Vec<Piece>>` (children hold `Rc<Fragment>` as today). `Piece::Rect(BoxId)` marks where a box records its geometry — it replaces `hit_order.push` and is what the hit traversal reads. `ChildRef` loses `at` and `hit_at`.
- [ ] **Task 2: the writers.** Every `list.items.push` becomes `list.pieces.push(Piece::Item(..))`; `emit_at` pushes `Piece::Child(..)`. `insert_item` (background behind children — "the box model emits the children first and inserts the background behind them") becomes an insert at a remembered `pieces.len()` with NO index fixing, because there is nothing to fix.
- [ ] **Task 3: the readers.** `walk_items`/`walk`/`iter`/`materialized` walk `pieces`. `geometry()` derives hit order from `Piece::Rect` in traversal order. `BeginClip`/`EndClip` lose `filhos_antes`/`filhos_dentro`; `rts-egui`'s clip handling is checked (one grep) and adjusted if it read them.
- [ ] **Task 4: the two index-fixers.** `empilhamento.rs` prepends the negative-z list as pieces — `pieces.splice(0..0, negativos)` — and the whole index-correction block is deleted. `relativo::desloca_desde` shifts pieces from a remembered length; the `c.at >= desde` filter dies.
- [ ] **Task 5: the stitch.** `costurar` replaces a `Piece::Child` in place, by `caixa`, instead of by position in `children`. `sujeira_coberta` reads the children out of the pieces.
- [ ] **Task 6: the rulers**, plus the paint dump: `cmp` silent over the local pages. If a page differs, the diff is a paint-ORDER defect that the old arithmetic had and this reveals — name it, measure it in the Blink, and decide (fix in this sub-lot only if the fix is the deletion; otherwise `esperado-a-falhar` with the reason).

### What comes after, and is NOT this lot

- **SC** — painting recursively per stacking context is a traversal over `pieces`, which is why it waits for BT-2b.
- **USED** — the used value of a box is read from its fragment.
- **`BeginClip.node`, `ScrollRegion.node_idx`, `grid_column_tracks` by node, `IntrinsicWidthKey.node`** — each is a consumer that asks by `NodeId` from outside (`rts-egui` scroll, `computedProperty`); they stay by node until that consumer is given a box, and this plan names them so they are not assumed done.
- **`stacking_key` walking the DOM** — an anonymous box has no node style (I6); the SC lot owns it.

## Ordering rationale

BT-2a before BT-2b because BT-2b touches the same 11 files and every `Option` unwrap in them would have to be ported twice. BT-2b before BT-2c because "one rect per fragment" needs the stitch to identify a child by box, not by position — otherwise a fragment replaced mid-sequence shifts every `at` after it, which is exactly the class BT-2b deletes.
