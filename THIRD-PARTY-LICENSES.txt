RTS — third-party licence inventory
===================================

GENERATED FILE. Do not edit by hand.

Regenerate with:

    cargo install cargo-license          # once
    cargo license --avoid-dev-deps --avoid-build-deps > THIRD-PARTY-LICENSES.txt

Refresh it whenever Cargo.lock changes. The reasoning, the obligations, and the
compiler-vs-runtime split are in THIRD-PARTY-NOTICES.md; this file is the data
that document is about.

Read `A OR B` as a choice RTS makes (Apache-2.0 or MIT is always taken where
offered) and `A AND B` as terms that all apply at once.
